# CRSP POS — Cashier quick guide

## Start of day
1. Open the CRSP POS app / browser page on the till. Tap **POS / Cashier**.
2. Type your **PIN** and press **OK**.
3. Count the cash in the drawer. Type the amount and tap **Open shift**.
4. Staff who only need to mark attendance: on the PIN screen tap **Clock in / out**, type PIN.

## Taking an order
1. Pick the order type at the top: **Dine-in** (enter table), **Takeaway**, **Delivery** (phone, address, area), **Drive-thru**, **Online**.
2. Tap a category on the left, then tap an item. If it asks for choices (spice level, sauces) pick them and tap **Add**.
3. Wrong item? Use **−** in the order list. Special request? Type it in the **Note** box.
4. Tap **PAY**.

## Paying
- **Cash:** tap **Exact**, or type what the customer gave. The screen shows the change. Tap **Complete order**.
- **Card / JazzCash / Easypaisa:** choose the method, type the approval or transaction number from the machine/app, **Complete order**.
- **Raast / bank transfer:** type the reference number. The order is marked *pending verification* until a manager checks the money arrived.
- **Split bill:** on the PAY screen tap ÷2 / ÷3 / ÷4, or **By items**, then take each person's payment.
- The receipt prints by itself and the kitchen gets its ticket. Tap **New order**.

## Discounts, hold, void
- **%** button: give a discount. Above your limit the screen asks for a **manager PIN**.
- **Hold** puts the order aside; **Held** brings it back (you can bring several back together).
- **Orders** (top): today's orders. **Void** cancels a whole order, **Refund** returns money. Both need a reason and a manager PIN.

## If the internet goes down
Keep working. The top bar says **Offline** and shows how many sales are waiting. Cash, card (typed code) and wallet sales are saved on the till and print as normal. Discounts, points and cash-on-delivery are not available until the internet is back. When it returns the sales upload by themselves. Do **not** clear the browser data on the till while sales are waiting.

## Delivery
Choose **Delivery**, enter phone, address and area. Pay now, or tap **Cash on delivery**. A manager assigns a rider on **Deliveries**. When the rider comes back, open **Deliveries** and tap the rider's name under *Riders holding cash* to count and receive the money.

## End of day
Tap **Shift**, then **Close shift**. Count the drawer and type the total (the screen does not show the expected amount first). It then tells you if you are over or short. Sign out.

## Locked screen
The screen locks after a few minutes of no use. Type your own PIN to continue; your order is still there.
